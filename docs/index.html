Questspector takes character data exported by the Fallen London Sheetifier browser extension and uses it to generate a rough summary of your character's current progress in the game's storylines. Just upload you CSV file below to begin.

Summary generation takes place client side within your browser. Your file won't leave your device.

Quests don't render until character data has been uploaded. Head to the UPLOAD tab to get started.
